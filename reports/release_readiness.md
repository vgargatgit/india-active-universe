# Release readiness

`india_equity_data_v1.14.1` has official NSE daily observation coverage from 2006-01-02 through 2026-08-10, compressed Parquet artifacts, a raw source manifest for 5,084 files, effective-dated identity rows, issuer/listing-episode artifacts, corporate actions with split, bonus, and cash-dividend fields, full PIT liquidity features, terminal-event classifications, non-overlapping status intervals, delisting notice evidence, page-level and event-level suspension evidence, corporate-action boundary findings, and PIT validation output. Remaining limitations are explicit: event completeness is not claimed, most adjusted rows remain `RAW_ONLY` or `PARTIALLY_ADJUSTED`, two exact-identity suspension starts are represented as bounded status intervals, while unresolved notices remain evidence, scanned notices need OCR, BSE cross-reference data is absent, and terminal values are unknown unless documented. The release is `DATASET_EXPLORATORY`, not `RESEARCH_HIGH_CONFIDENCE`.
