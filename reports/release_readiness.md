# Release readiness

`india_equity_data_v1.3.0` has official NSE daily observation coverage from 2006-01-02 through 2026-08-10, compressed Parquet artifacts, a raw source manifest for 5,084 files, effective-dated identity rows, issuer/listing-episode artifacts, corporate actions with split and bonus factors, terminal-event classifications, status intervals, delisting notice evidence, conservative page-level suspension evidence, and PIT validation output. Remaining limitations are explicit: event completeness is not claimed, most adjusted rows remain `RAW_ONLY`, suspension evidence needs event-level identity review, scanned notices need OCR, dividend/total-return adjustment is not complete, and terminal values are unknown unless documented. The release is `DATASET_EXPLORATORY`, not `RESEARCH_HIGH_CONFIDENCE`.
