# Corporate-action quality

Release `india_equity_data_v0.3.0` contains 39,185 official NSE EQ corporate-action records covering 2006 onward. 29,708 records link to canonical securities by ISIN; 9,477 remain in the identity-review queue. Unambiguous bonus ratios are applied to research-adjusted prices. Split, dividend, rights, merger, demerger, and scheme records remain event evidence until a verified adjustment factor is available.
